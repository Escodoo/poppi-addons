Customizes the purchase order and request for quotation reports:

- Adds a "Desc. Poppi" column (internal reference and product name) next
  to the supplier description.
- Shows the ICMS and IPI tax names in the taxes column and the IPI total
  in the totals block.
- Shows the warehouse CNPJ/CPF and the planned date, and prints the
  purchase order in landscape.

Usage
=====

To use this module, you need to:

1. Go to *Purchase > Orders > Requests for Quotation* and open a record.
2. Print the *Purchase Order* or *Request for Quotation* report.
3. The report shows the customized columns, taxes and totals.
