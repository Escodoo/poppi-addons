Customizes the purchase order and request for quotation reports:

- Adds a "Desc. Poppi" column (internal reference and product name) next to the
  supplier description.
- Shows the ICMS and IPI tax names in the taxes column and the IPI total in the
  totals block.
- Shows the warehouse CNPJ/CPF and the planned date, and prints the purchase
  order in landscape.
